Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Maux ("Maux Music", "Maux Server", "we", "our") is designed from the ground up to respect your digital sovereignty and personal privacy. We believe your music collection, listening habits, and library data belong entirely to you.

1. Overview & Self-Hosted Architecture

Maux Server and its companion client applications are self-hosted software. When you run Maux Server, all audio files, playback history, metadata, and user accounts are hosted and stored on your own hardware or infrastructure.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not track, collect, or upload your browsing, playback, or music streaming habits.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: Maux does not bundle Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDKs, or any advertising trackers.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Sale: We never sell, rent, or monetize any user data.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Server

The following data is maintained strictly within your self-hosted SQLite database or client local storage:

  • User Accounts & Passwords: Credentials hashed using secure algorithms (e.g. Scrypt) and break-glass recovery tokens.
  • Audio Library Metadata: Artist, album, track names, lyrics, cover art, and local file paths.
  • Playback History & Favorites: Your play counts, playlists, likes/dislikes, and queue states.

4. Third-Party Authentication (Optional)

If you explicitly enable optional third-party authentication such as Google Sign-In / OIDC, data exchange is strictly limited to authenticating your identity:

  • Google Sign-In / OIDC: When signing in with Google, Maux requests only basic profile information (your email address, display name, and profile picture URL) to authenticate your user account. Maux never requests sensitive scopes, does not access your Google Drive or external account contents, and never receives or stores your raw Google account password.

5. Network Permissions & Local Network Access

Maux client applications (iOS, macOS, Android, Windows, Linux) require local network access exclusively to discover and communicate directly with your self-hosted Maux Music Server instance via LAN or configured HTTPS endpoints.

6. Security

Maux uses strong, industry-standard encryption, cryptographic server identity verification, and scoped access tokens to protect communication between your devices and your self-hosted server.

7. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or Maux's privacy practices, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: dot-Mixah/Maux-Music-Server.
